# Roadmap

This file is the plan of cluster-design. The Status page of the site reads it, and the tracker of the project mirrors it.

| ID | Item | Status | Summary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | The first package: parts and fit rules | done | Part 1: `@mark1russell7/cluster-design` has the part classes as data (`PART_CLASSES`), the machines of a design, and 19 fit rules as Vex programs. A rule that cannot read a part gives `unread`, not a pass. Part 2: the site is a live demo of the check, with the power measures of a machine as Vex programs. |
| R2 | The formulas of a machine | done | The watts at idle and under load, the heat, the noise and the capacity of one machine. Each formula has units from `@mark1russell7/quantity`. Part 1: 9 new measures as Vex programs in `src/formulas.ts`, the class `fan`, 3 new fit rules, and tiles of the measures in the demo. Part 2: a test holds that each measure of each table has a unit of `@mark1russell7/quantity`. The test found 3 measures of backup power with an unknown unit. The fix gives them the units `USD/{charge}` and `{rank}`. |
| R3 | The site and its limits | done | A site as data: the circuits, the outlets, the space, the limits of heat and noise, the price of electricity. The formulas give the load of each circuit against its limit. Part 1: the record `site/1`, with volts, amperes and `USD/kWh` from `@mark1russell7/quantity` 0.4.0. A site has 5 rules and 13 measures as Vex programs, at the levels site, circuit and room. Part 2: the demo puts copies of its machine on a site of two circuits and two rooms. It shows the load, the heat and the noise against each limit, the cost for a month and each rule of the site. The address keeps the site. |
| R4 | The design space | done | A design as a configuration of `@mark1russell7/strategy-space`, so a search can go through the designs. The rules of R1 and the limits of R3 are its hard constraints. Part 1: `designSpace` makes the space of a catalog. The fit rules of one and two classes give its constraints, each with its reason, and two property tests hold that the constraints are sound. `checkDesignConfig` gives one verdict for a config, from the fit rules and the rules of the site. Part 2: the free places and the free sockets of a site lower the max of the copies, each limit with its reason, and a property test over random sites holds them. Part 3: `sweepDesigns` sweeps a grid or a seeded sample through `@mark1russell7/strategy-search` 0.0.1. Each design gets one row, with the rules that it breaks, four objectives and its Pareto rank. A rule without a read gives no rank. A property test holds that each row has the verdict of the check. |
| R5 | The cost of a design | done | The price of the parts to buy, and the cost of the electricity over a horizon. Part 1: the price list `prices/1` is input data, for example from `bestOffer` of `@mark1russell7/market`. 5 cost measures as Vex programs give the price of a machine, the purchase, the cost of the electricity, the total cost and the cost of a CI job slot. The purchase takes the parts of the stock first, and the owned machines need no part. A sweep can minimize the total cost. Part 2: the demo shows the price of the machine, the parts to buy, the cost of the electricity for 3 years, the total and the cost of one CI slot. A slider sets the time at full power, and the address keeps it. |
| R6 | An interactive view | done | A page shows the space of the designs and the values of one design. Part 1: the demo sweeps a seeded sample of 250 to 1000 designs in a worker of the browser. The designs have the parts of the demo and the site of the demo. A chart shows two goals of 6, with the front of those goals and the designs that break a rule. A visitor chooses a design to see its values, its parts, its places and the rules that break. One click opens the design in the builder. The address keeps the goals, the count and the seed. The live page fits at 1280 and 390 px, in light and dark. |
| R7 | Backup power | done | The record `backup/1`: the loads and the batteries of a site. 3 rules and 14 measures as Vex programs give the load of each battery, its minutes, its full charges, its price of a minute and its rank in its need. The calibration gives the usable share and the own use of a battery from two runtime points of its maker. Property tests hold the calibration, the rank and the scale of the energy. |
| R8 | The first release to npm | done | The package gets its build (`tsdown`), its `publishConfig`, its API report and its license, and it loses `private`. The release needs the bootstrap of a new package (`RELEASING.md`). Version 0.1.4 is on npm since 2026-11-15, with provenance. |
| R9 | A genetic search of the designs | done | `evolveDesigns` searches the design space with `evolve` of `@mark1russell7/strategy-search` 0.0.3, with the evaluation of `sweepDesigns`. A study in CI (`study.yml`) compares its front recall with a seeded sample of the same count of evaluations, over 10 seeds. The first study (2026-11-15) found a front of only 3 vectors. The search found each vector in each seed at budgets 200 and 400. The sample found 0.30 of the front at 200 and 0.67 at 400. At 400, the difference is within 2 SD of the sample. The second study (2026-11-15) uses the space with the split of work (`--space split`), with a front of 21 vectors. At budget 400 the search finds 0.83 of the front, and the sample finds 0.04. The search is higher in 10 of 10 seeds. `SOURCE_LOAD_LOW` gives the rule of the low end of the loads of backup power. |
| R10 | A mix of work | in progress | A machine runs other work next to its CI jobs: sessions, workers of quick checks, services and labs. Part 1: the option `work` gives the classes of work, each with its threads and its memory. The work lines of a machine give the units of each class. The work lines take their share first, and `runner-slots` counts the CI jobs in the rest. 2 new fit rules tell if the work lines fit. `workMeasures` and `siteWorkMeasures` give the units and the capacity of each class. Part 2: the option `split` of the design space gives the range of the units of each work class on the new machine. Each range is one stage, so a sweep or a genetic search finds the split. `workObjectives` gives the units of each class as objectives. The field `demand` of a class gives the units that the site needs, and the site rule `demand-<id>` holds it. Thus a search keeps only the splits that hold each demand. Part 3: the machine builder of the demo site has steppers for sessions and services, and two presets of work. The count of CI jobs falls as the work takes its share. Part 4: the view of the design space has a split of work. Each new machine gets 0 to 8 sessions and 0 to 2 services, and the units of each class are two more goals. |
